## What is implemented

### Patient journey and clinical handoffs

- Appointment slot booking and check-in produce a linked OPD visit and doctor-specific daily serial; duplicate/conflicting actions and doctor scope are checked.
- Queue calling prevents simultaneous active patients for the same doctor. Start/finalize actions use permissions, preserve clinical state across payments, and finalize prescription handoffs atomically.
- IPD conversion preserves the patient and visit link. Admission/bed transfer/discharge lock relevant rows; admission numbers are hospital-wide unique.
- IPD clinical/nursing notes, vitals, medication administration, handovers, monitoring and medicine orders, bed transfer, discharge request/approval, department clearance, final billing and discharge have been connected.
- Non-billable nursing/diet/monitoring tasks do not block financial clearance. Medication administration updates the due task instead of leaving a second unfinished entry. Cross-admission medication references and writes to closed admissions are rejected.
- Discharge requires explicit approval, summary and clearances. Authorized overrides require a reason. Department clearance endpoints reject unfinished corresponding orders.
- Pharmacy dispensing rejects nonpositive quantities and negative prices, checks patient/visit consistency, and locks prescription/stock records before deduction.

### Barcodes and PA

- Replaced invented barcode drawings with JsBarcode Code 128/Code 39. Independent ZXing decoding tests round-trip patient, OPD, invoice, mixed-case secure token and card identifiers.
- Scanner results enforce expected patient/type, branch and clinical scope, reject unsafe mismatches, and carry query parameters to the exact appointment/visit/invoice or order workbench. Stock batches derive branch scope from their inventory item.
- Invoice barcode encodes the invoice number; it no longer combines two identifiers into an unresolvable string. Print previews and refund/return vouchers retain barcode navigation to the original invoice.
- Azure Speech synthesis runs on the backend. English and Bangla neural voice configuration, bounded caching, sequential audio playback, fallback labeling and cancellation are present. See `docs/OPD_MODULE.md` and `backend/.env.example`.

### Billing operations

- Open `/billing/operations` from the invoice desk. It provides custom billing types/departments, default catalog-item mappings, saved financial report snapshots, cashier balance and cash handover/acknowledgment.
- Invoice creation snapshots item prices, item/invoice discounts, classification and creator. New discounted invoices require a discount reason/approval reference. Item defaults and invoice classification remain separate from clinical source links.
- The catalog GET previously returned flushed but uncommitted identifiers. They disappeared between requests. Catalog IDs now persist; synchronization is serialized for concurrent requests.
- `/billing/invoices/{id}/history` exposes application-append-only financial snapshots with actor, time, reason, before/after values and item details for billing mutations.
- Controlled invoice corrections allow note, classification and invoice discount changes with a required reason and before/after history. Prices/quantities of original lines are retained. Reductions below collected money are rejected; financial edits after a refund/return are blocked. Administrative corrections require `billing.service.manage`.
- Returns validate cumulative previously returned quantity and credit the discounted amount. A returned charge no longer becomes new unpaid debt. Original invoice totals remain visible; return credits and cash payouts remain separate records.
- Return/refund vouchers can be previewed and printed from the invoice history. Refunds record their actual payout method; cash reconciliation excludes card/bank/mobile payouts.
- Cash handovers include each unhanded cash receipt/refund once, record expected/count/variance and notes, and require acknowledgment by the designated independent supervisor. Receiver eligibility is displayed by name.
- Operational report snapshots persist filters, generation time, totals, creator and department breakdowns and receipt methods. Current report dates select invoice creation dates in UTC. Mixed-invoice department allocations use frozen item gross-charge weights and reconcile to the invoice totals. These are invoice-cohort reports, not a cash-received-on-day report or a complete general ledger.

### GUI and shared states

- Billing entry now follows patient → items → review/payment; removed duplicate KPI strips and reduced item columns. New registration, patient history, referral and internal notes use disclosures.
- Billing detail shows creator/classification and corrections/history; payment/refund/return/receipt panels are disclosures. Returns/refunds are accessible from paid invoice details, not just the due queue.
- Mobile toolbar wraps; IPD admission table scrolls inside its container; assistant is moved above fixed submit actions; registration heading contrast is corrected; toast stack is bounded.

## Physical medicine returns and five-role follow-up — 2026-11-01

This section supersedes the earlier statement that the unified physical dispense-return path is unimplemented.

### Implemented physical workflow

- Migration `20261101_0066` adds a persisted return history to each dispense; `20261101_0067` repairs the legacy stock-transaction table's missing active flag. Both were applied locally. The existing Base-model-only test schema had not exposed that legacy schema mismatch; the actual API workflow did.
- Pharmacy selects an **original issue**, including its batch/store, confirms physical receipt/inspection and records a reason. A multi-batch issue requires a specific source; the API rejects over-returning either that source or the overall dispense. It locks the dispense while applying the return.
- Saleable returns reverse the actual original stock source: inventory batch, original store balance and aggregate inventory quantity, or the original legacy medicine ID/stock movement. Name matching is no longer used to decide where stock goes. Batch cost is restored with its quantity. Renaming a product cannot redirect the return into another product.
- The safe default is **not restocked**: damaged/unfit goods are recorded as physically received for disposal with no increase in saleable inventory. Expired batches cannot be restocked. Receipt history records source, actor, date, reason, disposition, quantity and stock-movement reference.
- Request identifiers make retries idempotent. A duplicate request cannot add stock twice. Legacy prior returns without enough source history are blocked for supervisor reconciliation instead of guessing a batch.
- Receiving goods does not itself pay money or reopen the prescription for another issue. Cashiers credit no more than the quantity pharmacy has physically received; billing credit/payout remains separately authorized and audited. Duplicate pending work created by walk-in billing is now consumed by the actual dispense.
- Return POST requires both `pharmacy.dispense` and `pharmacy.return`. The GUI requires receipt confirmation, source selection and reason, shows receipt history, defaults to disposal and prevents repeated clicks. Pharmacists without billing-view permission can load the workspace without an unauthorized invoice-list request stalling it.
